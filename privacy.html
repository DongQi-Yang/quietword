Quiet Word — Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

EnglishEspañolPortuguês

English

Quiet Word does not collect personal data. The developer receives no information about who you are or how you use the app.

Your notes stay yours. Bookmarks, highlights, notes and your reading and listening position are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, they are also kept in your own private iCloud storage so your devices stay in sync. The developer cannot see or access them. You can export them at any time from the Me tab.

No ads, no tracking. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking software, and it does not share data with third parties.

Audio and text downloads. To play or download Bible audio, the app requests files from our content server (hosted by Cloudflare). Like any web server, it receives your device's IP address as part of each request. We do not use it to identify or track you.

Purchases. Quiet Word Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; the developer receives no payment details.

Diagnostics. Apple may give the app reports about crashes or performance. They stay on your device and are only sent to the developer if you choose to share them yourself (Me → Export diagnostics).

Children. The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
